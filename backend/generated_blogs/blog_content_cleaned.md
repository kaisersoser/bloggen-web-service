## Key Considerations When Choosing

*   **Your Resolution and Refresh Rate:** Are you gaming at 1080p, 1440p, or 4K? What refresh rate does your monitor support? Higher resolutions and refresh rates demand more powerful GPUs.
*   **The Games You Play:** Graphically intensive AAA titles will push hardware harder than esports titles. Check benchmarks for the specific games you play most.
*   **Your Budget:** Be realistic about what you can afford. Sometimes, a slightly older generation part can offer better value if your budget is tight.
*   **Productivity Needs:** If you do video editing, 3D rendering, or other compute-intensive tasks, a CPU with more cores and a GPU with more VRAM will be beneficial.
*   **Power Supply and Case Compatibility:** Ensure your power supply unit (PSU) can handle the wattage requirements of your chosen CPU and GPU, and that they will physically fit in your PC case.
